# Dashboard

**Dashboard** is the first page after you sign in. It answers three questions: how many drones are up right now, which ones, and what your team changed recently.

<Screenshot id="fleet-manager/dashboard" alt="Fleet Manager dashboard with four stats cards, the UAV Status list and Recent Activity" caption="Dashboard: the four stats cards, UAV Status with battery and GPS per drone, and Recent Activity from the team." />

## Stats cards

| Card | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Total UAVs** | Drones paired to the organization you have open (*In this organization*). |
| **Online** | Drones in this organization connected right now, heard from within the last 20 seconds (*Currently connected*). |
| **Organizations** | Organizations you are a member of. |
| **Team Members** | Members of the organization you have open. |

Switch organization in the sidebar to see another organization's numbers.

## UAV Status

A list of your most recently seen drones with their status, battery and last check-in. Select a drone to open its [UAV page](./fleet/uav-details.mdx), or **View All** to go to [Fleet](./fleet/index.mdx).

## Getting Started

New organizations see a two-step checklist until both steps are done:

1. **Create or Join an Organization** (**Go to Team**)
2. **Pair a UAV (UAVcast)** (**Pair Device**), completed when the organization has at least one drone

Follow [Add your first drone](./pair-uav.mdx) for the second step.

## Recent Activity

The last 10 events in your organization, with who did them. Fleet Manager logs, among others:

- drones paired, re-paired and removed
- stream bindings created and removed, ground stations assigned
- members invited, removed and role changes, ownership transfers
- VPN enabled or disabled

The activity log is kept when a drone or member is removed, so you can still see who did what.
