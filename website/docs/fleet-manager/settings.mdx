# Settings, account and billing

Open **Settings** from the sidebar (**Organization → Settings**) or the user menu. It has four tabs: **Profile**, **Organizations**, **Support** and **History**.

## Profile

Your name, email, picture and password belong to your UAVmatrix account. **Profile changes are managed through the UAVMatrix Store**: under **Password & Security**, select **UAVMatrix Store** to change them there. Fleet Manager picks up the changes automatically.

## Organizations

Rename the organization, edit its description or (Owner only) delete it. See [Set up your organization](./set-up-organization.mdx#rename-or-delete-the-organization).

## Support

Your support tickets with their status, and the **Support Chat Bubble** switch that shows or hides the floating support button. See [Support and FleetBot](./support.mdx).

## History

Telemetry retention, pruning and per-drone history deletion, for Owners and Admins. See [Flight history](./map/flight-history.mdx#how-long-history-is-kept).

## Billing

Fleet Manager has no billing page. Licenses, device slots, orders and invoices are all in your account at [uavmatrix.com](https://uavmatrix.com). See [Licenses and seats](./licenses.mdx).

## Delete your account

**Profile → Danger Zone → Delete Account** deletes your Fleet Manager account. Type `DELETE` to confirm.

What it removes:

- your profile and account information in Fleet Manager
- all your organization memberships
- all your active sessions
- missions you created and invitations you sent

Entries you made in **Recent Activity** stay, without your name.

:::warning Owners must hand over first
You can't delete your account while you own an organization. Fleet Manager lists the organizations and asks you to [transfer ownership](./team/index.mdx#transfer-ownership) or delete them first.
:::

Deleting your Fleet Manager account doesn't delete your UAVmatrix store account or your licenses. Manage those at [uavmatrix.com](https://uavmatrix.com).
