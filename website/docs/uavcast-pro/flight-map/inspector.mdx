# MAVLink Inspector

The Inspector lists every MAVLink message UAVcast-Pro receives, grouped by vehicle, with its rate and latest field values. Use it to check that the flight controller sends what you expect, to find a message's exact field names, or to confirm that an [injector](../flight-controller/injectors.mdx) produces `NAMED_VALUE_FLOAT` messages. Open **Flight Map → Inspector**.

<Screenshot id="uavcast-pro/flight-map--inspector" alt="MAVLink Inspector with connection status and the message list for system 1 showing message names, rates in Hz and counts" caption="Flight Map → Inspector with the messages from the flight controller (system 1) and ATTITUDE expanded." />

## What you see

**MAVLink Connection Status** at the top: **Connection**, **Vehicle**, **GPS Satellites**, **Total Messages** and **System ID**.

**Message Inspector** below: one group per vehicle (*Vehicle (System ID: 1)*), and in it one row per message with:

- the message name,
- **COMP**: the component ID that sent it (1 = autopilot, 194 = the default for injectors),
- the rate in **Hz** and the number of messages received.

Open a message to see its fields and current values.

## Typical checks

| Question | Look for |
|---|---|
| Is the GPS sending data? | `GPS_RAW_INT` at 2 to 10 Hz, and `GPS_RTK` if you use [RTK](../flight-controller/ntrip-rtk.mdx). |
| Why is telemetry slow in the ground station? | Low rates on `ATTITUDE`, `VFR_HUD` or `GLOBAL_POSITION_INT`. Raise the `SRx_` stream rates on the flight controller's MAVLink port. |
| Does my injector work? | `NAMED_VALUE_FLOAT` under COMP 194 (or the compid you set). |

If the list says *Waiting for messages...*, the flight controller link isn't connected. See [Flight controller](../flight-controller/index.mdx).
