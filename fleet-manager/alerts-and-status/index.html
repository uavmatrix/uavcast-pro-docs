Status indicators and notifications

Fleet Manager shows problems where you are already looking: on the map, on the fleet list and on each drone's page. This page lists every indicator and what to do about it.

Drone indicators​

IndicatorWhereMeaningFirst thing to check
OFFLINE, grey marker with clock badgeFleet, map, UAV pageNothing received from UAVcast-Pro for 20 secondsThe drone's internet link and port 8883. See Drone offline in Fleet Manager.
NO MAVLINK, orange ring with !MapUAVcast-Pro is online but has had no flight controller data for 10 secondsFlight controller power and cable. See No MAVLink heartbeat.
Red dashed ring, NO GPSMapNo satellites or no fixAntenna view of the sky, GPS cable.
Yellow dashed ring, WEAK GPSMapFewer than 6 satellites or only a 2D fixWait for more satellites before flight.
Battery in yellow / redFleet cardsBelow 50 % / below 20 %Plan the landing.
ARMEDUAV page, control panel, map popupMotors armedTreat the aircraft as live.
FAILSAFEUAV page, control panelThe flight controller is in failsafeThe flight controller's messages, shown in the map popup and Logs.
GCS FAILSAFE / GCS Failsafe not enabledUAV page, control panelGround-station failsafe triggered, or not configuredSet FS_GCS_ENABLE before BVLOS flights.
Re-pair requiredFleet, UAV pageThe drone uses an old connection certificateRe-pair the drone.
Signal poorMap popupRound trip to the drone above 600 ms, or three missed pingsCellular coverage, or a congested link.

Indicators update live while the page is open. Fleet Manager checks for offline drones every 5 seconds.

Activity log​

Changes your team makes (pairing, removing drones, stream bindings, invitations, role changes, VPN) are logged under Recent Activity on the Dashboard, with who made them.

System announcements​

When UAVmatrix has planned maintenance or an incident, a banner appears at the top of every page: blue for information, amber for a warning, red for critical. Dismissing it hides it until you open Fleet Manager in a new browser session.

Status page​

System → Status (page System Status) shows whether Fleet Manager is working normally: All systems operational, or We're experiencing some issues with the Active Incidents. Past Incidents lists the last resolved ones with their Resolution. Check it first when many drones go offline at once.

Email and webhook alerts​

Fleet Manager doesn't send email, SMS or webhook alerts for drone events yet (for example low battery or a drone going offline). Keep the map or fleet list open in the control room, and rely on the flight controller's own failsafes for battery and link loss.
